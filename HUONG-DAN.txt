HANH KPI - ung dung PWA (ban 2)

A. CAI APP LEN DIEN THOAI
1. Dua thu muc nay (tru Code.gs) len hosting HTTPS mien phi (GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop, Cloudflare Pages...).
   Thu nhanh o may: python3 -m http.server 8080 trong thu muc nay, roi mo http://localhost:8080
2. Mo link tren dien thoai: Android (Chrome) menu > Cai dat ung dung; iPhone (Safari) Chia se > Them vao Man hinh chinh.
3. Chay offline sau lan mo dau tien. Du lieu luu trong may; nho "Tai file sao luu" o tab Cai dat.

B. DONG BO 2 CHIEU VOI GOOGLE SHEET (khoang 10-15 giay khi co mang)
1. Mo Google Sheet HANH 2026 > Tien ich mo rong > Apps Script.
2. Xoa code mac dinh, dan toan bo file Code.gs. Doi TOKEN (dong 9) thanh mat khau cua ban. Ten tab mac dinh la 'MIX 1 NEW'; neu ban doi ten tab thi sua SHEET (dong 8).
3. Trien khai > Trien khai moi > loai Ung dung web. Thuc thi voi tu cach: Toi. Quyen truy cap: Bat ky ai. Cap quyen khi duoc hoi.
4. Sao chep dia chi Web App (ket thuc bang /exec).
5. Trong app: Cai dat > Dong bo Google Sheet > dan dia chi + mat khau > Luu va dong bo.
Luu y: neu sua Code.gs sau nay, phai Trien khai > Quan ly trien khai > Sua > Phien ban moi.
Mat khau chi nam trong may ban va trong Code.gs, khong nam trong file sao luu.
